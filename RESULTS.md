# Result inventory

`studies/S03-regime-map/results/` is the immutable canonical evidence set:
every structured record the released analysis reads is here.

## Authoritative evidence

- 42 Layer-A raw cells: 21 independent-process and 21 continuous-batching slot
  measurements, plus two summaries.
- One 36-item accuracy probe across all three serving variants.
- 12 held-out tuning artifacts.
- 1,690 sweep episodes: B=810, C=420, E=340, H=120.
- 700 compressed per-episode LLM decision sidecars.
- 4,200 RQ2 repeated calls over 30 byte-identical states.
- One RQ2 determinism-control probe and the fixed sampled-state artifact.
- One RQ3 local deliberation-energy artifact.
- Three final real-hardware validation replays.
- 40 oracle-static evaluation episodes.
- Hosted and local pilot summaries.
- The cumulative hosted-spend ledger (`spend_ledger.json`): total recorded
  spend, per-model totals, and call count, appended by `src/llm_controllers.py`
  on every hosted controller call. This is the artifact behind the manuscript's
  recorded hosted spend of $14.86. Because it counts every hosted call the
  harness made — including pilot, probe, and retried calls — its total exceeds
  the $13.63 sum of the per-episode `usd` fields in the committed sweep and RQ2
  rows.

`src/verify_artifact.py` checks these counts, unique keys, strict JSON, all gzip
CRCs and metadata headers, and request conservation for every sweep row.

## Derived tables

`results/analysis/` contains:

- `cells.json`: every volatility/cadence/controller cell with means, n, and CIs.
- `margins.json`: paired LLM margins against the rule baseline envelope.
- `blocks_EH.json`: intent-change, self-triggered, and partial-intent results.
- `realised_I.json` and `overload_fraction.json`: realized workload properties.
- `rq2_summary.json`: byte/decision stability, entropy, and contradiction rates.
- `failure_modes.json` and `failure_modes_attempts.json`: episode- and
  attempt-level failures.
- `oracle_static_I1000.json`: volatility-informed robustness check.

Eight of these tables are regenerated by the default offline workflow. The
oracle-static table is separately checked by checksum, strict schema parsing,
and its 40-row raw evidence inventory.

## Reading the evidence

The sweep and RQ2 files are JSON Lines: one independently addressable record per
line. Decision sidecars are strict JSON arrays compressed with deterministic
gzip headers (`mtime=0`, no embedded filename). Records carry relative
experimental time rather than wall-clock timestamps, and model references use
stable GGUF filenames. Relative time, latency, energy, token, cost, action,
prompt, response, workload, and outcome fields are all present.
